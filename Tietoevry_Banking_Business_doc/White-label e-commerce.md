## Overview

Tietoevry's E-Commerce Payment Gateway is a **white-label payment solution** designed for e-commerce businesses that demand a fully branded, seamless checkout experience — without the complexity of building their own payment infrastructure.

> In short: merchants look like a payments company, without having to become one.

---

## What is a White-Label Payment Gateway?

A white-label payment gateway **decouples the customer-facing checkout experience from the underlying payment processing infrastructure**.

- **What the customer sees:** The merchant's own domain, UI, and branding — end to end.
- **What runs behind the scenes:** A battle-tested payment platform handling transaction routing, PCI compliance, tokenization, and acquirer integrations.

Unlike redirect-based payments (e.g., being sent to a PayPal or Stripe page), a white-label gateway **embeds the entire payment flow** within the merchant's environment.

## Why E-Commerce Companies Choose This Model

### 1. Brand Consistency & Trust

Payment is the most trust-sensitive moment in the user journey. Keeping users within the merchant's own brand environment eliminates trust leakage to third-party pages and reinforces customer confidence.

### 2. Higher Conversion Rates

Every redirect and unfamiliar brand logo increases checkout abandonment. An embedded, branded payment flow reduces friction and measurably improves conversion.

### 3. Customer Loyalty & Data Ownership

When users interact with the merchant's brand at every touchpoint — including payment — trust, behavioral data, and long-term loyalty remain with the merchant, not a third-party provider.

### 4. Fast Time-to-Market

Merchants leverage Tietoevry's proven infrastructure (compliance, routing, settlement) and iterate freely on checkout UX without re-architecting the payment core.
