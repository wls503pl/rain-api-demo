# Tietoevry Banking Card Business Summary: Operational Models and Market Positioning

## 1. Company Positioning and Scale

Tietoevry Banking is a specialized financial SaaS business unit under the Tietoevry Group (annual revenue approx. €3 billion, listed in Helsinki, Stockholm, and Oslo). ## 2. Core Product: Card Suite Platform

Card Suite is the central software platform providing full lifecycle management for card operations.

### 2.1 Functional Modules

- **Card Issuing:** Supports Debit, Credit, and Prepaid cards across major networks (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, JCB, UnionPay).
- **Authorization Processing:** Real-time transaction validation including balance checks, risk management rules, and PIN verification.
- **Clearing & Settlement:** Handles transaction clearing, fund settlement, and reconciliation.
- **Merchant Acquiring:** Secure, configurable, and compliant end-to-end acquisition processing.
- **Card Ledger:** A modern ledger system managing credit, prepaid, and cardless credit/deposits; handles fees, interest accrual, billing, and repayment tracking.
- **Fraud Prevention:** 24/7 real-time fraud monitoring services.
- **Dispute Management:** Standardized processes for disputes in compliance with card scheme rules and local regulations.
- **Switching:** Transaction routing and message transformation.

### 2.2 Technical Characteristics

- **Scheme Agnostic:** Supports international (Visa, MC, AmEx) and local card schemes (e.g., BankAxept, NPS/PROSTIR).
- **Multi-tenant Architecture:** Highly scalable, processing billions of transactions and millions of cards.
- **Standardized Protocols:** Uses ISO 8583 for transaction messaging and routing.
- **Compliance & Security:** Supports EMV 3-D Secure 2.x, PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication (SCA), and is PCI DSS certified.
- **Flexible Deployment:** Available via Private Cloud, Public Cloud, or On-premises.

## 3. Transaction Authorization and Lifecycle Management

### 3.1 Authorization Flow (Four-Party Model)

The system facilitates the standard flow: **Cardholder → Merchant → Acquirer → Card Scheme (Visa/MC) → Issuer (Tietoevry System)**.

As the technical service provider for the Issuer, the platform performs:

1. Card validity verification.
2. Balance/Credit limit checks.
3. Real-time fraud detection and risk rule execution.
4. PIN/3DS authentication.
5. Authorization response (Approve/Decline).

### 3.2 Card Issuing Lifecycle

The platform manages the complete journey from application to renewal:

- **Onboarding:** KYC/AML compliance checks.
- **Product Configuration:** Defining credit parameters, interest algorithms, and loyalty schemes.
- **Tokenization:** Supporting digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.) by replacing sensitive card data with unique digital identifiers.
- **Servicing:** Managing monthly billing, interest calculation, and dispute resolution.

## 4. Card Personalization and Specialized Services

Tietoevry offers advanced physical card manufacturing and personalization services with a focus on innovation and sustainability.

- **Sustainability:** Over 75% of cards (aiming for 90% in 2025) use sustainable materials like rPVC, rPET, or wood-based materials.
- **Premium & Innovative Products:** Metal cards, biometric (fingerprint) cards, and wearable payment devices (rings, bracelets).
- **Accessibility:** Cards designed with Braille, notches, or voice activation to comply with the European Accessibility Act.
- **Production Hubs:** Strategic centers in Finland, Latvia, and Norway for disaster recovery and regional support.

## 5. Market Trends and Strategic Evolution

### 5.1 Card System Modernization

Current industry data indicates a significant shift toward modernization:

- **80% of banks** are currently modernizing their card systems (up from 60% in 2023).
- **Legacy Burden:** Banks typically spend up to 70% of their IT budget on maintaining legacy systems.
- **Drivers:** Rapidly changing payment landscapes (BNPL, A2A), new customer demands (instant issuance, virtual cards), and regulatory requirements.

### 5.2 Business Model

The business operates primarily on a **B2B Enterprise Outsourcing** model:

- **SaaS:** Pay-per-usage model, removing the need for banks to build internal systems.
- **BPO (Business Process Outsourcing):** Comprehensive operations including 24/7 customer service and fraud monitoring.
- **Strategic Partnerships:** Long-term contracts (typically 5+ years) with major institutions like Kaspi.kz, Bank Norwegian, and Handelsbanken.
